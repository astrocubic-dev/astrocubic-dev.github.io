Jipmongi Support

Questions or trouble using the app? Email support@astrocubic.com. Include your app version, the user number shown in Account, and when the issue occurred. Keep passwords, authentication tokens, and sensitive reading content out of the message.

My records are missing

Records are stored on your device. If you saved an iCloud backup, restore it under Settings → iCloud Backup and Restore. Signing back into your account retrieves your points balance. It does not automatically restore your full journal.

I want to delete my account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account and verify your identity. This removes your account, points, and server-stored answers. Delete local records and backup files separately. We keep the verification value and first award timestamp described in the privacy policy to prevent repeat claims of starter points after re-registration.
