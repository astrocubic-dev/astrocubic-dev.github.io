Jipmongi Privacy Policy

ASTROCUBIC · Effective November 9, 2026

Who operates the app

ASTROCUBIC operates Jipmongi. This policy covers profiles, journal entries, sign-in, AI readings, and points. Contact us at support@astrocubic.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores local profiles, doll and decoration preferences, optional birth details, worries and questions, card and folk-tool results, reading answers and follow-ups, notes, and ritual entries on your device. Rituals involving a named person are symbolic in-app activities. They do not send messages or notifications to that person. We do not collect physical or biometric measurements.

Accounts and points

If you sign in with Apple, we use the account identifier and the email address Apple provides, including a relay address if you choose Hide My Email. If you sign up or sign in by email, we use the email address you enter for authentication. Sign-in tokens are stored in protected device storage. We associate your points balance, reading request IDs, types, statuses, timestamps and costs, completed AI answers, and points credits with your account. An eligible Apple account can receive 30 starter points once. Deleting the account and signing up again does not renew the offer. Local profiles are separate from your sign-in account and share that account’s points. For point purchases, we process transaction information needed to confirm payment and credit points.

Information sent for AI readings

When you consent and request an AI reading, we send your question, selected cards and deck, observation results, and relevant earlier reading content through our server to an external AI provider. Profile names, dates of birth, and private notes are not automatically included. If you type names, contact details, or other personal information into a question or earlier reading, that information may be sent as part of the request. Please avoid entering sensitive information. Withdrawing consent disables new AI readings. You can still use the cultural experiences and view saved entries.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for server authentication and storage and Anthropic’s Claude API for AI readings. The server is configured in Australia. AI requests may be processed in the United States or elsewhere in the provider’s processing environment. These transfers are used for authentication, points transactions, and generating readings. Transfers are encrypted. The providers’ policies govern how they retain or delete AI input and operational logs. We do not share data with these providers for advertising. See the Supabase Privacy Policy and Anthropic Commercial Terms.

Retention and deletion

On-device records remain until you delete them or remove the app. Account-linked answers and points history are kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete the account. Provider security logs and backup copies may follow the providers’ retention policies. Request IDs and statuses are used to investigate technical errors. We do not intentionally include the text of questions, answers, or authentication keys in app diagnostic logs. To prevent repeat starter-point claims, we retain a one-way value derived from the Apple identifier using a server secret, along with the first award timestamp, while the offer operates. This value survives account deletion and is stored without the original Apple identifier, email address, name, or reading content. We do not treat it as anonymous data. It is used only to check eligibility after re-registration.

Deleting your account and local records

After verifying your identity, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account to delete your account, points balance, server-stored answers, and points usage and award history. If you used Sign in with Apple, that connection is revoked. Local profiles and records are not automatically synchronized with the server account. You can delete them separately in Settings → Records and Data. Other people’s profiles on the device are not automatically deleted. The verification value and first award timestamp described above are retained for the stated purpose and period.

iCloud backup and restore

You can save a backup file manually or enable automatic backups and choose a destination folder. Backups include local profiles, decorations, records, and notes. They do not include sign-in information, account user numbers, or points balances. If you remove the app or lose the device before backing up, you may lose local records. Signing into the same account lets you retrieve the points balance. Full reading history is not restored automatically. Use the Files app to remove backup files. Restoring an older backup may bring back records that were saved at the time.

Permissions, tracking, and support

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. It handles shake gestures through system events while open. It does not use advertising tracking or advertising or analytics SDKs. Viewing notices, authenticating, and requesting readings use the internet. Hidden-notice preferences are stored on the device. Support emails are sent only when you review and send them.

Your choices and policy updates

You can withdraw permission under AI Reading Information, delete individual entries or the current local profile’s records, reset the app, or delete your account. For access, correction, deletion, or other requests, email support@astrocubic.com. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive reading content. If our data practices change materially, we will update the app and this policy and request consent where needed.
