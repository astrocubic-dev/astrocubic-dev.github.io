How it works

See tonight's sky in three steps

  1. Choose a date and time.View the Moon and planetary layout for any moment you want to explore.
  2. Choose Solar system or Observed sky.Compare the full orbital layout with a horizon-based view.
  3. Accelerate time.Watch planets, moons, and the lunar phase move through time.

Location is only for observing the sky

Current location permission is used only to calculate the sky direction and horizon. You can change permission in Settings or choose a simulated city at any time.
