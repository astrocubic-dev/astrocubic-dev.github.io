Compare the Prediction With the Real Sky.

Obstruction is predicted from available terrain and structure data. Missing geometry or heights, trees, wires, balconies and site conditions can change the real view. Weather recommendations are separate from overall observing scores; missing forecasts are not treated as clear skies.
